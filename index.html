How to Use

Simple 5-Step Application Guide

Follow these steps to get maximum results from B.S. Organic Manure on your farm.

1

Test Your Soil

Before applying, do a basic soil test to understand current nutrient levels and pH balance.

2

Calculate Quantity

Use our Fertilizer Calculator to determine the exact number of bags needed per acre.

3

Prepare the Field

Plough or till the field. For best results, apply manure 2–3 weeks before sowing seeds.

4

Spread Evenly

Spread manure uniformly at the calculated rate. Mix lightly into the top 6 inches of soil.

5

Irrigate & Monitor

Water the field after application. Monitor growth and reapply at mid-season for best results.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your manure 100% chemical-free?▼

Yes. All our products are made purely from organic matter — crop residue, cattle waste, and worm-processed material. No synthetic additives or chemicals used at any stage.

How much manure do I need per acre?▼

It depends on your crop and soil condition. Generally, 200–500 kg per acre is recommended. Use our Fertilizer Calculator on this page for an accurate estimate.

Do you deliver across India?▼

Yes! We deliver to 18 states across India. For major cities, delivery takes 3–5 business days. Bulk orders above 500kg go via truck directly to your location.

What is your return policy?▼

We offer a 7-day return policy for damaged or incorrect products. For quality complaints, we conduct a lab test and offer replacement or full refund.

Can I get a sample before bulk ordering?▼

Yes! We provide 1kg sample packs for ₹99 (adjustable against your first bulk order). Contact us via WhatsApp to request a sample.

Do you have dealer / distributor programs?▼

Absolutely! We're actively looking for dealers across India. Check our Dealer Registration section or contact us directly for margin structure and terms.
